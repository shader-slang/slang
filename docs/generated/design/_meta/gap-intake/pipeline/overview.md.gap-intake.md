# Gap-intake report for pipeline/overview.md

## Summary

Nothing was escalated: no observation in this queue contradicted what
the watched source says the compiler should do. Four gaps were fixed,
two rejected as bogus, one rejected as out of scope, and one deferred.
The most consequential finding is that the block-parameter gap's
suggested addition was wrong: `constructSSA` sits in the mandatory
pass block of `generateIRForTranslationUnit` and runs at every
optimization level, so block parameters are _not_ established by an
optimization pass; what the test actually observed is that
`eliminatePhis` takes the IR back out of SSA form before every
back-end, and that an `OpPhi` in emitted SPIR-V is reconstructed by
the downstream SPIR-V optimizer, which runs only above optimization
level `None`. The document now says that instead of the hypothesis.
The two `cascading-only-mention` gaps asked for "tested in" pointers
that the document already carries, and the `#include` example belongs
to the per-stage page.
