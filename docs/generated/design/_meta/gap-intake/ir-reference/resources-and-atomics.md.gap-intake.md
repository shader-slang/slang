# Gap-intake report for ir-reference/resources-and-atomics.md

## Summary

This is a re-run of the gap-intake stage for this page. The previous
cycle fixed seven of ten gaps and deferred three — `f742df1cbf5e`,
`925a5389cc94` and `7ef0acfec3ad` — on the mistaken basis that no
`slangc` was available to try candidate shaders. A native arm64
`slangc` at `build-arm64/Debug/bin/slangc` settles all three, so this
cycle carries the seven earlier verdicts forward verbatim and moves the
three deferrals to `fixed`. The queue is now ten fixed, zero rejected,
zero deferred, zero escalated.

What the compiler showed changed the answers, not just supplied them.
For `f742df1cbf5e` the gap's premise was wrong: a single scalar
entry-point uniform does not fold, it produces the SBT lookup; what
suppresses the lookup is declaring the uniform at module scope, or
using a `compute` stage. For `925a5389cc94` the missing surface turned
out to be a _capability_, `-capability spvDescriptorHeapEXT`, and the
two heap opcodes are core-module intrinsics present from linking
onward rather than things SPIR-V legalization invents, which the table
implied. For `7ef0acfec3ad` the gap's proposed answer (`nointerpolation`
alone) was incomplete: the callable surface is `GetAttributeAtVertex`,
and which of the two opcodes appears is a target decision.
